# Part 2 - B7: Participate in CS/DS/cybersecurity clubs’ activity.

### Activty: UWA's Data Science Club's "Data Science Industry Insights"
Coordinated by UWA's Data Science Club, this event gave students the opportunity to hear directly from industry professionals about what it's really like to work in the field of data science. Through guest speakers, followed by a Q&A session, we gained insights into the experience of working in professional data science environments. The event also provided a networking oppurtunity, allowing students to connect with and build relationships with industry professionals and like-minded peers.
